What's inside

Simple to learn, satisfying to master

🧩

Solver-verified levels

Every level is checked for a fair, solvable shuffle and a known optimal move count — no dead ends, no impossible boards.

⭐

3-star challenge

Beat the optimal move count for three stars, then come back and shave your solution down to perfect.

📈

Live "moves to go" HUD

A running counter shows the optimal remaining moves as you play — always know how close you are to a clean solve.

💡

Hints when you're stuck

A built-in solver offers one-step and three-step hints, plus reference views (pip, side, or ghost overlay) to guide you without spoiling it.

🎓

Training mode

A guided coach walks you through moves with animated arrows — perfect for learning the ropes on a new grid size.

📴

Play anywhere

No internet, no account, no cloud sync. Your progress, stars, and personal bests are saved locally on your device.

How to play

You'll get it in seconds

1

Pick a world and a level. The picture is scrambled into a grid with one open space.

2

Tap or swipe a tile next to the gap to slide it into place. Multi-slide lets you move a whole row or column at once.

3

Watch the "moves to go" counter tick down as the picture comes back together.

4

Solve within the level's optimal move count for all three stars, then unlock the next world.

Built private by design

Shiffle collects no personal data, has no servers, shows no ads, and includes no in-app purchases. Everything you do stays on your device.
