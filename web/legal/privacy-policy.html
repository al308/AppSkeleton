Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Summary in plain language Shiffle does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We have no servers. The app contains no ads and no in-app purchases. Your game data (level progress, stars, settings) lives only on your device and is never shared with anyone.

1. Who We Are

Shiffle is a mobile puzzle game developed and published by Alex Lemken ("we", "us", "our"). If you have questions about this policy, contact us at alexlemken@googlemail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not collect gameplay analytics or usage statistics ourselves.
  • We do not transmit any data to our own servers — we have none.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app uses your device's local storage (AsyncStorage) to save the following information exclusively on your device:

  • Your world and level progress, stars earned, and personal best move counts
  • Settings such as haptics, multi-slide, control mode, and reference display

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. No Advertising, No In-App Purchases

Shiffle v1.0 contains no advertisements and no in-app purchases. We do not integrate any ad SDK or monetisation framework. You will never be shown ads.

5. Children's Privacy

Shiffle is suitable for a general audience. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). Because we collect no personal data at all, the app is fully compliant with COPPA and GDPR-K.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including:

  • Right of access — You can request a copy of any personal data we hold (we hold none).
  • Right to erasure — You can uninstall the app to delete all locally stored data.
  • Right to lodge a complaint — You can contact your local data protection authority.

As we do not process any personal data, GDPR obligations are minimal. All game data is stored exclusively on your device and never transmitted to us or any third party.

7. Data Security

All data handled by the app is stored locally on your device using the device's built-in storage mechanisms. We do not transmit any data over the internet. There are no servers to breach.

8. Third-Party Services

Shiffle v1.0 does not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect or process personal data. There are no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services embedded in this version.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
